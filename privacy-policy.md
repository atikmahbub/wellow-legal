# Wellow Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 25 October 2026

Wellow ("the app", "we", "us") is a habit and wellbeing tracker. This policy explains what information Wellow handles, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

**The short version:** your habits, check-ins and notes are stored on your phone. Nothing leaves your device unless you use a feature that needs it (weather today; backup and AI summaries once they're available), and we never sell your data or show ads.

---

## 1. Information stored on your device

By default, everything you enter stays in a database on your phone:

- Habits you create, their schedules, reminders and completion history
- Check-ins: mood, symptoms (for example chest tightness), how strong they were, possible triggers and any notes you write
- Calm and breathing sessions
- App settings, such as your name for greetings and your theme

This data is not sent to us. If you uninstall the app without a backup, it is deleted with the app.

Home screen widgets read a summary of this data (for example today's habits and progress) from storage on your device. They don't send it anywhere.

## 2. Information that leaves your device

### Weather and air quality (optional)
If you allow location access, Wellow uses your **approximate** location to show temperature, humidity and air quality.

- Your coordinates are rounded on your phone to about 1 km before they are sent, so your exact location never leaves the device.
- The rounded location is sent to our server, which fetches conditions from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms). Results are kept briefly in memory per area so we don't have to fetch them again, and they are not linked to you or stored in a database.
- To show your area's name (for example "Mirpur, Dhaka"), the app asks your phone's built-in location service to look up the same rounded location: Apple on iPhone, Google on Android. The name is shown in the app and isn't sent to us.
- When you save a check-in, the conditions at that time (temperature, humidity, air quality) are saved with it **on your device**.

You can turn off location access at any time in your phone's settings. Everything else keeps working.

### Anonymous device ID
To make sure only the app can use our server, and to prevent abuse with rate limits, the app registers with a random anonymous ID. This is the RevenueCat app user ID if you have used purchases, or otherwise a random ID created on install. It isn't linked to your name, email or Google account.

### Account and backup (optional, Wellow Pro, coming soon)
*This section applies once backup is available in the app.* You will be able to sign in with Google to back up your data and sync it between devices.

- **What we receive:** a Google account identifier and your email address, which Google gives us when you sign in. We don't receive your Google password.
- **What we store:** a copy of your Wellow data (habits, completion history, check-ins, calm sessions and settings) so it can be restored on another device.
- **Why:** only to provide backup and sync.

### AI weekly summaries (optional, Wellow Pro, coming soon)
*This section applies once AI summaries are available in the app.* If you ask for an AI summary of your week, the app sends **calculated statistics only**: completion percentage, habit names with streak lengths, the number of tight days, top triggers as percentages, the number of calm sessions, and average mood per day.

- Your notes and individual check-ins are **not** sent.
- These statistics are processed by an AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) to write the summary. Under their API terms, these providers do not use the data to train their models.
- We record how many summaries you have requested so we can apply the weekly limit. We don't store the content of the summaries on our server.

### Purchases (Wellow Pro)
Subscriptions are handled by **Google Play** and our subscription service, **RevenueCat**. They process your purchase and tell us whether Pro is active. We never see your payment details.

- RevenueCat assigns an anonymous app user ID and keeps a record of your purchases.
- Our server keeps whether your Pro access is active and when it expires.

See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## 3. What we don't do

- We don't sell or rent your data.
- We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
- We don't use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- We don't share your data with anyone except the service providers named above, and only for the purposes described above.

## 4. Health information and medical disclaimer

Check-ins can include information about how you feel physically, such as chest tightness or shortness of breath. We treat this as sensitive:

- It stays on your device unless you turn on backup.
- It is never used for advertising and never sold.

Wellow shows patterns, not diagnoses. **It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.** The doctor report PDF is created on your phone, and is only shared if you choose to share it yourself. If you have symptoms that worry you, contact a doctor or your local emergency number.

## 5. Notifications

If you turn on habit reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. We don't use notification content to profile you.

## 6. Security

Data sent between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Account tokens are stored in your phone's secure storage. Access to our server is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is less to protect.

## 7. How long we keep data

| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| Data on your device | Until you delete it or uninstall the app |
| Backup data and account | Until you delete your account |
| Anonymous device ID and Pro status | While you use the app, so access can be checked |
| AI summary usage counts | A rolling 7 days for the weekly limit, and deleted with your account |
| Weather lookups | Only briefly, in server memory, by area. Never linked to you |

## 8. Deleting your data

- **On your device:** delete individual entries in the app, or uninstall the app.
- **Data on our server:** email us (below) to ask for deletion, and we'll delete it within 30 days. Once sign-in is available, you'll also be able to delete your account in the app. This permanently deletes your account, backed-up data, Pro status record and AI usage records straight away.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel it in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions**.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is processed. Most of your data is already under your control on your device. For anything stored on our server, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.

## 10. Children

Wellow is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app.

# Delete your Wellow data

This page explains how to delete your data from **Wellow**, the habit and wellbeing tracker by **Atik Mahbub**.

## Data on your phone

Your habits, check-ins, notes and calm sessions are stored on your phone. You can delete:

- **Individual entries:** delete them in the app.
- **Everything:** uninstall Wellow, or go to **Android Settings → Apps → Wellow → Storage → Clear storage**.

Data on your phone is never sent to us, so there's nothing else to request.

## Data on our server

Our server stores a small amount of data linked to your device: an anonymous device ID, your Wellow Pro status and, once those features are available, your account, backup data and AI summary usage.

To delete it:

1. Email **[atikmahbub100@gmail.com](mailto:atikmahbub100@gmail.com?subject=Wellow%20data%20deletion%20request)** with the subject **"Wellow data deletion request"**.
2. Include the email address of your Google account if you've signed in. If you haven't, include your Wellow Pro order number from Google Play (it starts with `GPA.`) so we can find your records.
3. We'll confirm and delete your data within **30 days**.

Once sign-in is available, you'll also be able to delete your account straight away in the app.

## What is deleted

| Data | Deleted |
|---|---|
| Account (Google account ID and email) | Yes, permanently |
| Backed-up habits, check-ins, calm sessions and settings | Yes, permanently |
| Pro status record and anonymous device ID | Yes, permanently |
| AI summary usage counts | Yes, permanently |

## What is kept

- **Purchase records** are kept by Google Play and our subscription provider RevenueCat, as required for billing, tax and legal reasons. We can't delete these.
- **Deleting your data doesn't cancel your subscription.** Cancel it in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.
